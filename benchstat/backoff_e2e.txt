Phase I new-engine benchmarks (post steering probe-buffer fix, b5e8e89).
count=5; GenBackoff at -cpu=4, EndToEndAllConfigs/backoff at -cpu=24.

Reading the numbers against the closest previous quality setup
(interned/XXH3 at ~141ms e2e, ~14µs/gen indexed micro):
- e2e (build + one gen): backoff ~1.0s vs 141ms — ~7x, one corpus pass per
  order (6 tables). B/op 484Mi is transient build churn, not residency.
- gen micro (10k gens/op): plain 124.9ms = ~12.5µs/gen (multi-order probe
  stack alone, ~same as a single-order chain plus backoff probes); steered
  2.89s = ~289µs/gen — the per-candidate steering probes at wide order-1
  contexts dominate. Allocation is flat vs plain (~37 allocs/gen) after
  hoisting the probe buffer; the remaining cost is cache misses, not GC.
- What steering buys for that: zero verbatim 6-grams in 1,000 sentences
  (TestSteeringOracle) with no generate-reject-retry loop and no validator
  to wire; interned/XXH3 only rejects after generating a full sentence.
All comfortably inside the deployment budget (a handful of gens per day).
